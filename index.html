    GAME STATION / PUI FORTUNE

    Your journey guide.

    Goal

    After two hours, finish the current round. Your total wealth is cash + property purchase values + your building values. Highest total wins.

    On your turn

    Click your animal token, choose 1–6 steps, then move clockwise and resolve the space. Passing Start earns $200. Buy an open property, pay rent on a rival’s property, or draw a Chance card.

    Landmarks & upgrades

    Any team may buy an unowned Landmark it lands on after correctly guessing its country. An owner may add one City Upgrade to each Landmark for half its purchase price. An upgrade doubles that Landmark’s base rent and adds its cost to your final wealth.

    Rent or challenge

    Land on a rival’s landmark and you choose: pay the rent, or challenge the owner to a mini game played away from the screen. If the owner wins you pay double rent. If you win you pay nothing. Record the result honestly — it cannot be undone.

    Stops along the route

    Income Tax costs $100. City Maintenance Tax costs $50. On a Transit Station, pay $40 to travel to the other station and end your turn there. Free Parking is a breather.

    Jail

    If sent to Jail, on your next turn either pay $50, use a Get Out of Jail pass, or miss one turn. Landing on Jail is just visiting.

    Trading & cash

    Teams may trade Landmarks and money whenever all involved teams agree. If a forced payment exceeds your cash, pay what you have and leave the game. Your Landmarks return to the bank.
