    City Fortune rulebook

    Play the city your way.

    Goal

    After two hours, finish the current round. Your total wealth is cash + property purchase values + your building values. Highest total wins.

    On your turn

    Roll one die, move clockwise, and resolve the space. Passing Start earns $200. Buy an open property, pay rent on a rival’s property, or draw a Chance card.

    Properties & upgrades

    Any group may buy an unowned property it lands on. An owner may add one City Upgrade to each property for half its purchase price. An upgrade doubles that property’s base rent and adds its cost to your final wealth.

    City spaces

    Income Tax costs $100. City Maintenance Tax costs $50. On a Transit Station, pay $40 to travel to the other station and end your turn there. Free Parking is a breather.

    Jail

    If sent to Jail, on your next turn either pay $50, use a Get Out of Jail pass, or miss one turn. Landing on Jail is just visiting.

    Trading & cash

    Groups may trade properties and money whenever all involved groups agree. If a payment makes cash negative, record the debt and continue: it counts against final wealth.
